Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email kalitaventures@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Kapi Plus. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

I got a new phone. Where is my data?

Kapi stores data on your device, so it moves with your iCloud or computer backup. Restore the backup to the new phone. You can also export a CSV in Settings.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check Settings > Notifications > Kapi on your device, and make sure dose reminders are on in Kapi's Settings. Reminders are scheduled from your last logged dose, so log each dose to keep them on time.

How is the estimated level calculated?

From the doses you log and published half-life and time-to-peak averages for your medication. It is an estimate for orientation, not a lab value.

Restore a purchase

On the plans screen or in Settings, tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.
