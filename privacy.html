Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Kapi does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Kapi stores

Kapi stores what you enter: your first name, medication, dose and schedule, the doses you log (date, dose, injection site, pain level, notes), weigh-ins, height and goal weight, and daily check-ins (side effects, appetite, energy, protein, water, notes). All of it is saved in a local database on your iPhone or iPad and is included in your regular device backups according to your own iOS settings.

What Kapi does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No servers. Kapi has no backend and never uploads your data.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and no third-party code that sends data anywhere.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Kapi reads your step count and body weight to show your activity and progress, and can write the weights you log. This data is read and written on your device through Apple's HealthKit framework. It is never sent to Kapi or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising. You can turn off access at any time in the Health app.

Notifications

Dose and check-in reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Estimated levels

Medication-level charts are calculated on your device from the doses you log and published pharmacokinetic averages. No data is sent anywhere to produce them.

Purchases

Kapi Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and gives Kapi only a signed receipt confirming your subscription. Kapi never sees your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Exports

When you export your data as a CSV file, it leaves the app only through the iOS share sheet to the destination you choose.

Children

Kapi is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data wipes the local database. Deleting the app removes all app data from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
